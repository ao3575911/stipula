# Abstract

People make promises, pick versions of a file, and close decisions inside Telegram, then cannot point at a durable record without exporting the whole chat. Stipula seals the exact bytes the bot observed, links each seal to the previous one in that chat, and lets other members witness the card.

The signature is the seal card: a short code, a content hash, a chain hash, and a witness button. Verification is a replay of the original bytes, not a claim that a court or a blockchain saw them.

The useful core is one hash chain. Commitments and decisions are seals of a canonical record, so the same audit covers notes, promises, and tallies.
